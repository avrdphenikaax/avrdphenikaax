01 / RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

Scientific questions.
Driving applications.

Ongoing and planned research across learning, reconstruction, planning, control and data. We seek clear scientific contributions and measurable value for autonomous driving.

01

Temporal learning & asynchronous inference

Understanding how differences in data sampling, training sequences and inference timing affect end-to-end driving. We explore temporal representations and asynchronous execution for consistent decisions under real-time constraints.

02

Efficient end-to-end architectures

Improving driving performance within on-vehicle compute and memory budgets. We study temporal and multi-camera representations, efficient architectures, and the trade-offs between driving quality, inference speed and sensor-to-action latency.

03

4D reconstruction & simulation

Improving geometry, appearance and motion in reconstructed driving scenes, including small objects, occlusions and dynamic road users. We investigate how detail and temporal consistency support more faithful simulation and driving evaluation.

04

Diverse trajectories & policy learning

Generating varied, dynamically feasible driving trajectories and improving policies through reinforcement learning. We explore learning from driving data, reward design and evaluation methods that reduce dependence on costly simulation.

05

Learning-assisted vehicle control

Investigating reinforcement learning to adapt model predictive control weights to changing driving conditions. Evaluation considers tracking accuracy, comfort, robustness and real-time computation within vehicle dynamics and operating constraints.

06

Vision-language-action driving models

Connecting visual observations, contextual reasoning and driving actions. We study temporal grounding, efficient inference and how reasoning can contribute to end-to-end driving decisions in complex traffic.

07

Driving datasets & unified benchmarks

Developing a research dataset covering long-tail situations in motorcycle-rich Asian traffic. We aim to connect perception, motion, reasoning and action through consistent annotations, data selection and benchmarks, with a path toward public release.

08

Closed-loop evaluation & generalization

Evaluating model behaviour beyond recorded-data metrics. We study scenario coverage, failure analysis and generalization across traffic conditions, using simulation and controlled vehicle experiments to assess the consequences of driving decisions.
